Selected Agora Essays

Reading the gap between institutional design and institutional reality

Starting with medical institutions and extending to public administration, energy policy, transport, aviation, and market systems, George Kujo asks why institutions persist after drifting from their purposes, who bears the costs, and who can correct their mistakes. His essays draw on primary sources, statistics, institutional documents, and public data.
