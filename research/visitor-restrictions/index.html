Hospital Visitor Restrictions

An inquiry into proportionality, patient rights, family presence, institutional discretion, and the point at which emergency restrictions should expire.

Research question

How should hospitals justify, review, and terminate restrictions on family presence after the emergency phase has ended?

Why it matters

Rules introduced as temporary safeguards can persist without a clear evidentiary threshold, review date, or route for affected families to challenge them.

Policy and guidance reviewRights and proportionality analysisCitation audit
