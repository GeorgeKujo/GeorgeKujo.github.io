COVID-19 Policy Review

A retrospective examination of evidence quality, emergency governance, institutional inertia, and exit mechanisms.

Research question

Which emergency policies remained proportionate as evidence changed, and which lacked a workable exit mechanism?

Why it matters

Retrospective review is necessary to distinguish measures that were justified by uncertainty from systems maintained after that justification weakened.
